What to have ready when you call

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • A short description of what is wrong and when it started.
  • Whether the main water valve is closed.
  • Whether anyone is at risk from electricity, gas or standing water.
  • A good callback number in case the call drops.

A clear description lets the dispatcher choose the right plumber and tools, and it helps us give you a clear price before work starts.
