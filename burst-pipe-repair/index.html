Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Lompoc, CA

A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons through a house in an hour. Shut the water off, then call the 24/7 line for a licensed plumber.

24/7 dispatchCopper, PEX and galvanizedSame-day when available

A burst or cracked pipe is a true emergency because water keeps flowing until someone closes a valve. The 24/7 line is answered at night, on weekends and on holidays, and a licensed plumber can usually be booked for the same day when availability allows.

Do the first part yourself. Find the main shutoff valve, which is usually at the front of the house, in a garage wall or beside the meter, and turn it fully off. Then open a low faucet to drain pressure and call (805) 249-7135. The dispatcher will ask where the water is and whether any of it is near outlets.

The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

  1. Close the main valve

    Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not turn, call us and the city water division.

  2. Cut power to wet areas

    Switch off breakers for any room where water is near outlets or fixtures. Do not step in standing water to reach a panel.

  3. Drain the lines

    Open the lowest faucet and flush toilets once to relieve pressure and drain the system.

  4. Protect belongings

    Lift rugs and electronics, and place buckets. Take photos for your insurer before you clean up.

  5. Call the 24/7 line

    Tell us the pipe type if you know it, where the water is and whether the main valve closed.

Why pipes fail on the Central Coast

Lompoc is not a hard-freeze market. The record low is 20 degrees, and snow is virtually unknown, so frozen-pipe bursts are the exception. The usual causes here are age and wear, high water pressure, corrosion at fittings and ground or framing movement.

That has a practical meaning. When a pipe bursts in a Lompoc house it is more often a sign of a worn system than a cold snap, so we look at the pipes on either side of the break and check your pressure. If several sections are thin or pitted, fixing only the visible break can mean another call within months.

The city's 2025 water report lists a pH near 8.4 and sulfate near 424 ppm, and the water is hard. Those minerals do not cause bursts by themselves, but scale-covered joints and pinholes are common on older runs.

When the problem is a drain pipe and not pressurized supply, the call may start as a burst pipe but finish as sewer line repair or drain work.

Repair or repipe: when one fix is not enough

A single failure in a pipe that is otherwise sound is a repair. Two or three failures in the same system within a year is a pattern. At that point the numbers favor repiping, especially with galvanized lines that rust from the inside.

We explain what we see in the walls, show you the pipe if we can and give you a clear price for repair and for replacement. You decide, not the dispatcher.

Water damage, drying and your insurance

Many homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage from a burst pipe, and they may not cover the pipe itself. Your insurer decides what applies, so call them early and keep photos, receipts and a record of what was wet.

We stop the leak and repair the pipe. Drying, mold control and drywall are handled by a restoration company, and we can work around theirs. If wet drywall is the first sign of the break, our leak detection page explains how we confirm where the water started.
