Tankless Water Heater Installation and Service in Lompoc, CA

Endless hot water is the promise. Correct venting, a sized gas line and regular descaling are what make it real.

Install, repair, flushHard water descalingGas and electric

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows instead of storing it in a tank. That saves floor space and avoids standby heat loss, but it also asks more of the house. The gas line has to be large enough, the vent has to be the right material and the heat exchanger has to be kept free of scale.

Flush and descale: the service people skip

Scale is the leading reason tankless heaters fail early, and it is a bigger factor in hard water. The City of Lompoc's 2025 water report lists average hardness near 293 ppm, about 17 grains per gallon. Water that hard coats the heat exchanger and narrows its passages.

Descaling circulates a mild acid through the unit using isolation valves, which should be installed at the beginning. The result is better flow and fewer error codes. Many manufacturers recommend yearly flushing in hard water, and we follow the maker's schedule for your model. Skipping it can void a warranty.

If your tankless unit has started showing error codes, shutting off after a few seconds or giving lukewarm water, scale is the first suspect. Call and tell us the code.

Venting: why stainless matters on the coast

Condensing tankless units produce acidic condensate and use plastic or stainless vent pipe depending on the model. Non-condensing units run hotter and need stainless steel or a rated special vent. Mixing materials or using the wrong one is a safety problem.

Lompoc sits near the coast, where fog and salt air are part of daily life. Outdoor units and exposed vent terminations deal with that moisture year-round, so we choose materials rated for it and check clearances to windows and doors. Always follow the maker's listed vent options.

Outdoor and indoor installs

Outdoor-rated units go on an exterior wall, freeing the garage or closet. Lompoc's mild climate, with a record low of 20 degrees, makes outdoor installs practical, though freeze protection is still worth discussing for the rare cold night.

Indoor units need a vent route and combustion air. Electric tankless units need heavy electrical service, often larger than a typical panel has spare. Either fuel type requires a permit in Lompoc where a system is replaced, so we confirm that with the building division.

Repair: what usually goes wrong

Error code or shutdown

Scale, a clogged inlet filter, a vent problem or a failed sensor.

Lukewarm water

Flow too low to trigger the burner, or a scaled heat exchanger.

Burner will not light

Gas supply, igniter or flame sensor trouble.

Cold sandwich effect

Residual cold water between uses. A recirculation line can help.

If the unit is older than its warranty and the heat exchanger has failed, replacement may cost less than repair. For a tank heater you want to repair, see water heater repair.

Safety checklist for a tankless water heater

  • Install a carbon monoxide alarm near the unit and the bedrooms.
  • Do not use or store flammable liquids near a gas heater.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 before calling a plumber.
  • Do not reset a vent error code repeatedly. Turn the unit off and call.
  • Keep the vent termination clear of leaves, nests and stored items.
