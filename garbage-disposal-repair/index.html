Turn the disposal off at the switch. If it is leaking, switch off the breaker too and put a bowl under the unit. Do not put your hand in. Call (805) 249-7135 and tell us the brand and whether it is humming or silent.

Why disposals jam, hum or stop

Hums but does not spin

Something is wedged in the grinding chamber or the flywheel is stuck.

Silent and no hum

A tripped reset button, a tripped breaker or a burned-out motor.

Leaks from the bottom

A cracked housing or worn seals, which means replacement.

Leaks at the sink flange

A failed mounting gasket or putty that can be reseated.

Leaks at the drain pipe

A loose slip nut or worn gasket on the discharge.

Slow draining sink

A clog beyond the disposal in the trap or the branch line.

Safe reset steps before you call

  1. Power off

    Switch off the disposal and the breaker if you plan to look inside.

  2. Free the flywheel

    Use the hex socket under the unit and work it back and forth with the right wrench.

  3. Remove the object

    Use tongs, never your hand.

  4. Press the reset

    The red button on the bottom after the motor has cooled.

If it trips again, the motor has failed. If the unit leaks from the body, stop here and call.

Grease, scale and why kitchen lines back up

A disposal grinds food but does not remove grease. Fat flushed down the sink cools and sticks to the pipe wall beyond the disposal, and the City of Lompoc stresses keeping fats, oils and grease out of its sewer system because they cause blockages downstream.

That matters for your own pipes. A disposal that seems to be the problem is often a kitchen branch clogged past it, and replacing the disposal does not help. Hard water, with Lompoc averaging near 293 ppm in the city's 2025 report, also leaves scale on the drain flange and gaskets over the years. Clearing the line is the right fix, and drain cleaning covers that.

What not to put in a disposal

  • Grease, oils and fat, even in small amounts.
  • Fibrous foods such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves in large amounts.
  • Coffee grounds in volume, which clump in the trap.
  • Pasta, rice and potato peels, which swell into a paste.
  • Bones, fruit pits and non-food items.

Run cold water for fifteen seconds after grinding. Cold water keeps remaining fat solid so it moves through the trap instead of coating it.
