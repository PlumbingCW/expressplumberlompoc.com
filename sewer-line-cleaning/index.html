Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Lompoc, CA

A blocked main line shuts down every drain in the house. We clear it, confirm it with a camera and tell you how to keep it open.

Main line cablingRoot cutting24/7 dispatch

When a main sewer line blocks, every toilet, sink and tub in the house is affected, and the lowest drain fills first. Treat it as urgent and call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber can usually be booked for the same day when availability allows.

Until we arrive, stop using water. Do not pour chemicals into the lines. If a floor drain or tub is already rising, put towels around it and keep people away. Call (805) 249-7135 and say how many fixtures are backing up.

Main line blocked: how it shows up

  • Several drains slow or stopped at the same time.
  • Water rises in a tub or floor drain when the toilet is flushed or the washer drains.
  • Gurgling in a toilet or tub when another fixture runs.
  • Sewage smell in the house or around the cleanout.
  • A previous clearing that held for only a few weeks or months.

Repeated main line stoppages are the signal that cleaning alone is not enough. A camera inspection tells you why. Our sewer inspection page explains what the video shows.

What we use to clean a sewer line

A main line cable machine sends a spinning cable with cutting heads through the cleanout. Different heads handle soft grease, packed paper and roots. We start with the lightest head and step up so the pipe is not damaged.

After the cable is out, a camera confirms the line is clear and shows what caused the blockage. That is worth seeing because a clean pipe and a broken pipe can both drain after cabling for a few days.

When grease coats the pipe, a cable punches a hole and the line closes again. That is the moment to consider hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall with high pressure water instead of boring a channel through the middle.

The City of Lompoc states that roots are a common cause of overflows and prohibits chemical root control products in the sanitary sewer. If you see a root killer in a hardware aisle, the city's rule is a reason to walk past it.

Your cleanout and the city line

The City of Lompoc says the property owner is responsible for the lateral, cleanouts and maintenance. The cleanout is the access point we use. If you do not know where yours is, we find it, and if there is none, we can discuss installing one, which makes every future cleaning cheaper.

Restaurants and food businesses face stricter rules. The city says new food service establishments must install a grease trap or interceptor meeting the most recent California Plumbing Code. Our commercial plumbing page covers that side.

After the line is clear

A cleared line still needs follow-up when it has clogged more than once. Ask for a camera view, request a written note of what was found and consider a maintenance schedule. Lines with roots often need cleaning once a year, and lines with grease may need jetting every few years depending on use.

If the camera finds a cracked or collapsed section, we explain repair options, and that conversation moves to sewer line repair. Your Vandenberg Village neighbors served by their community services district can use the same approach, since the cleanout is still on private property.

What to do and what not to do during a sewer backup

  • Stop using water, including flushing and the washer, as soon as a drain backs up.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into a backed-up line, because they sit in standing sewage.
  • Keep children and pets away from the backed-up fixtures.
  • Do not touch standing sewage without gloves and boots, and wash well afterward.
  • Photograph damage for your insurer before cleanup starts.
