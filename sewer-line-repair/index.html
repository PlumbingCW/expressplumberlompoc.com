Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Lompoc, CA

Sewage in a tub or yard is a health hazard, not a nuisance. We find the failure with a camera and explain every repair option.

A sewer backup is the plumbing problem most likely to need help within hours. If wastewater is rising in the lowest drain or you can smell sewage in the yard, stop using water in the house and call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber can usually be booked for the same day when availability allows.

While you wait, do not flush, run the dishwasher or start a load of laundry, and keep children and pets away from any standing wastewater. Then call (805) 249-7135 and tell us where the backup shows and whether your neighbors are affected.

Sewer backup: what to do first

  1. Stop adding water

    No flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is cleared.

  2. Find the cleanout

    A capped pipe near the foundation or in a side yard. Do not open it if the line is full.

  3. Check the other drains

    If every fixture is affected, the main is blocked. If only one, it is a branch.

  4. Keep people clear

    Stay out of wet areas and keep pets away. Wear boots and gloves if you must enter.

  5. Call the 24/7 line

    We will confirm whether a cable can clear it or the pipe needs repair.

Signs the sewer line, not the drain, is failing

A blockage can be cleared. A failed pipe cannot. These signs suggest the pipe itself is damaged.

Repeat backups

A line cleared every few months that clogs again.

Wet or sunken lawn

Water or a depression above the lateral between the house and the street.

Sewage smell outdoors

Odor near the cleanout, the yard or the foundation.

Roots on the camera

Fine hair roots or a root mass at a joint.

Offset or cracked pipe

A camera view of a bellied, collapsed or separated section.

Cleaning solves a clog. Repair solves a pipe that has broken, shifted or grown shut. If the line is simply greasy, hydro jetting is the right first step instead.

Who is responsible for the lateral in Lompoc

The City of Lompoc states that lateral sewer clogs, cleanouts and maintenance are the property owner's responsibility. The lateral runs from your house to the city main, and a failure on it is yours to fix. Where it connects to the city main is the city's responsibility, and the wastewater division can help you locate the connection point.

The city also says a sanitary sewer overflow violates the municipal code and must be reported, and that you should contact the wastewater division before digging on your property. In Vandenberg Village, sewer service comes from the Vandenberg Village Community Service District, which the city page lists for residents there.

That is why the first useful step on a bad night is a camera. It shows whether the failure is on your side of the property line or at the main.

Roots, cast iron and clay in older sewers

Roots enter at joints and cracks looking for water, then grow into a mass that catches paper and waste. Cutting roots clears the line, but the opening stays. Lining or replacement seals it.

Older homes may have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails differently. Cast iron corrodes from the inside, clay cracks and separates at joints, and Orangeburg can deform and collapse. We identify the material on camera and base the recommendation on it. For a cleaning without repair, see sewer line cleaning.
