Plumber Service Area Around Lompoc, CA

Lompoc, Vandenberg Village, Mission Hills, Santa Maria and the 101 corridor, all on one 24/7 line.

Lompoc Express Plumbing is a 24/7 emergency plumber line for the Lompoc Valley and the surrounding Central Coast. The area below is where we plan most appointments. If your address is not listed, call anyway. The dispatcher can tell you in a minute whether a plumber can reach you.

Lompoc and the valley

Lompoc is the heart of the service area, with ZIP codes 93436 and 93437 covering most addresses and 93438 serving PO boxes. Streets near the North H Street corridor and downtown mix commercial and residential plumbing, and neighborhoods around the city hold homes from many decades. Calls from this core area get the shortest routes and the best chance of a same-day appointment.

For water-related questions, the City of Lompoc publishes a yearly water quality report. According to the 2025 report, average hardness is 293 ppm, which is why scale shows up in so many water heater repair visits and why softener and filter questions are common.

Vandenberg Village and Mission Hills

Vandenberg Village and Mission Hills sit just north of the city. Vandenberg Village is served by the Vandenberg Village Community Service District, and that matters when a leak is near the meter. If water is running in the street side of the meter box, the district is the agency to call. If it is on the house side, the leak is yours to fix, and we can help.

Homes in these communities range from mid-century construction to newer builds. Ask for a leak detection visit if your water bill has crept up without a change in habits.

Vandenberg Space Force Base

Vandenberg Space Force Base is the largest employer in the area, and many customers live or work on or near it. Base access is controlled, so give the dispatcher the address, the gate you will use and any entry requirements when you call. Planned work such as water heater installation is easier to schedule than a late-night call, but emergencies are still answered.

Santa Maria and the Highway 101 corridor

Santa Maria is about thirty minutes away by road and has its own page, emergency plumber in Santa Maria, covering ZIP codes 93454, 93455 and 93458. Its water is harder than Lompoc's, and the page explains how that affects heaters and drains.

Buellton, Solvang and Los Alamos lie along US 101 and State Route 246. We do not keep separate pages for them, but the 24/7 line takes calls from all three, and the dispatcher will tell you when a plumber can arrive.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and nearest cross street.
  • Whether the address is a house, apartment, shop or on base.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have shut off the main valve.
  • Whether anyone is at risk from electricity, gas or slippery floors.

With that, the dispatcher can decide how urgent it is and which plumber to send. A clear price comes before any work starts.
