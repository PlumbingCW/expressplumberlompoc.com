Sizing, location and venting

A four-person household is usually comfortable with a 40 to 50 gallon tank, while a larger family or a big soaking tub may need more. A heater sized too small runs out. One sized too large wastes heat.

Location matters for service access. Garages are common in Lompoc homes and give the easiest installs, but gas units need combustion air and a safe distance from stored items. Closets and attics need proper venting and a drain pan with a line to a safe discharge. Moving a heater means new gas, water and vent lines, which is a larger job than a swap.

Permit, code and earthquake strapping

The Lompoc building division says a permit is needed before work on plumbing, gas and mechanical systems, and that replacing systems counts. The counter is open weekdays, and you can call it to confirm what your project needs. We pull the permit where required and schedule the inspection.

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires new and replacement water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped, at a minimum in line with the California Plumbing Code. The 2025 edition of Title 24 has an effective date of January 1, 2026. We strap every heater we install.

Hard water and your new heater

The City of Lompoc's 2025 water quality report lists average hardness near 293 ppm, or about 17 grains per gallon, with total dissolved solids near 817 ppm. Scale from water that hard shortens the life of any heater, and tank units benefit from a yearly flush.

If you want to protect a new heater, ask about a softener or scale control at install, since fitting it at the same time costs less than adding it later. Our water softener and filtration page explains the options.

What to do before the installer arrives

  • Clear a path and the space around the old heater.
  • Know where the gas shutoff and the cold water valve are.
  • Do not run hot water once the old tank has been turned off to drain.
  • Keep children and pets away from the work area.
  • Ask for the new heater to be braced, anchored or strapped.
