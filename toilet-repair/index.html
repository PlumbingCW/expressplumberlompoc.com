Emergency Toilet Repair in Lompoc, CA

An overflowing or leaking toilet needs a fast fix. We repair fill valves, flanges, wax seals and cracked bowls, day or night.

Overflow and leak callsRepair or replace24/7 dispatch

A toilet that is overflowing, rocking, leaking at the base or running endlessly is more than an annoyance, because water on a bathroom floor reaches the subfloor quickly. The 24/7 line takes these calls at night and on weekends, and a licensed plumber can often be booked for the same day when availability allows.

If it is overflowing, lift the tank lid, push the flapper down and close the shutoff valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise. Then call (805) 249-7135 and describe what the toilet is doing.

Toilet problems we see most

Overflowing bowl

A clog in the trap or branch line, or a failing fill valve that keeps feeding the tank.

Leak around the base

A worn wax ring, a loose closet bolt or a cracked flange.

Tank leaking onto the floor

Failed tank-to-bowl gasket or a cracked tank.

Running nonstop

A warped flapper, a mineral-stuck fill valve or a bad float.

Weak flush

Blocked rim jets or a partial clog, often scale in hard water.

Rocking toilet

A loose or broken closet flange or bolts.

Stop an overflow in thirty seconds

  1. Lift the lid

    Take the tank cover off and set it aside.

  2. Push the flapper down

    That stops water flowing into the bowl.

  3. Close the shutoff

    The oval valve behind the toilet, turned clockwise.

  4. Do not flush again

    Wait for the plumber if the bowl is still full.

If more than one fixture is backing up, you may have a main line problem and not a toilet problem, so see drain cleaning.

Cleaning a stubborn clog without damage

A flange plunger works better than a flat cup. Push slowly to break the seal, then pull with force. A closet auger reaches the trap without scratching porcelain. We carry both.

How to snake a clogged toilet by hand is a common homeowner question, and it is fine to try a plunger first. If a plunger and auger do not clear it, the clog is farther down, and a cable machine or a main line cleaning is the next step. Never use chemical cleaners in a toilet, because they sit in a full bowl and splash when you open it.

Hard water, scale and weak flushes

The City of Lompoc's 2025 water report shows average hardness near 293 ppm, or about 17 grains per gallon. Minerals build up in fill valves, flappers and the small holes under the rim, so a toilet that flushes weakly or runs on may be scaled rather than clogged.

That has a practical meaning. Replacing a fill valve and flapper is often enough, and cleaning rim jets restores flush power. If the porcelain itself is crazed or cracked, replacement is the better choice. A cracked bowl cannot be repaired reliably and should be replaced when it leaks.
