Check the water heater before it fails

A water heater usually gives warnings. Rumbling noises, rusty hot water, a damp base or a tank that runs out fast are all signs to have it checked. Homes of this era may still have the heater they started with, or a replacement from years ago, so look at the label for the date.

A water heater repair often solves a pilot, thermostat or valve problem. If the tank itself is leaking, repair will not help and a replacement is the answer.

A short routine that saves money

  • Twice a year, turn each shutoff valve under the sinks to confirm it still moves.
  • Once a year, flush the water heater to clear sediment, since Lompoc water is hard.
  • Replace worn supply hoses on washing machines before they burst.
  • Know where the main water shutoff is and test it.

Average hardness in the city's 2025 water report is 293 ppm. That is why aerators clog and heaters collect scale, so these small steps matter here.
