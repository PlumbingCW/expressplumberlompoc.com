When a clogged drain is an emergency

Not every clog needs a midnight call. A single slow bathroom sink can wait for a morning appointment. These situations should not wait.

  • Water or sewage rising in a tub, shower or floor drain when you use a toilet or the washer.
  • More than one fixture draining slowly at the same time, which points to the main line.
  • A kitchen sink that fills completely and will not move after a plunger and hot water.
  • Gurgling from toilets or drains, or a sewer smell inside the house.
  • Any overflow that is reaching carpet, hardwood or a shared wall.

Fixture-level clogs usually clear in one visit. Main-line stoppages can take longer because the cable has to reach past the cleanout and into the lateral.

Snaking, cabling and when to step up to jetting

A drain snake or cable machine is the right first tool for most stoppages. It bores through the blockage or hooks it so it can be pulled out. Smaller hand augers handle sinks and tubs, while a drum machine with a longer cable handles main lines.

When grease has coated the full pipe wall, a cable only punches a hole through it and the line clogs again. That is when hydro jetting makes sense, because high-pressure water scours the wall clean. If a camera shows a crushed or offset section, no amount of cleaning will fix it, and the conversation moves to sewer line repair.

Lompoc lateral lines, cleanouts and who is responsible

The City of Lompoc states that lateral sewer clogs, cleanouts and maintenance are the property owner's responsibility. The lateral is the pipe from your house to the city main. A cleanout is the capped pipe, usually in a side yard or near the foundation, that gives access to it.

That matters on a bad night. If water is backing up, the cable goes in through your cleanout, and the first minutes go faster if you already know where it is. The city also notes that roots are a common cause of overflows and prohibits chemical root control products in the sanitary sewer, so skip the foaming root killers and have the roots cut out mechanically.

If you plan to dig on your property after a repair, contact the city wastewater division first and call 811 before any trenching.

Preventing the next clog

Most repeat calls come from habits, not bad luck. Keep cooking fat out of the sink, run cold water while the disposal works, and use a hair catcher in the shower. Flush only toilet paper.

Lompoc water is hard, with the city's 2025 water quality report listing average hardness near 293 ppm, or about 17 grains per gallon. Hard water leaves soap scum that clings to pipe walls and narrows them over the years, which is one reason an annual cabling of a stubborn kitchen line is cheaper than a weekend call. Our leak detection visits also catch slow drain leaks that hide behind the same clog symptoms.
